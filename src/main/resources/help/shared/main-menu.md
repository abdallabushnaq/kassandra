### Navigate between planning pages

![Kassandra logo](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-logo.png)

The **Kassandra logo** identifies the application; use the menu entries to navigate.

![Main navigation](images/help/light/MainLayout/{{audience}}/main-layout-navigation.png)

The **main menu across the top** switches between the pages available to you.
Optional prototype pages can also appear when enabled by your administrator.

![Products menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-product-list.png)

**Products** is the starting point for finding a product and managing its access.
Enter through the intended product when the ownership of new work matters.

![Versions menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-version-list.png)

**Versions** groups a product's planned releases. Open the intended product's
version before creating features for it.

![Features menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-feature-list.png)

**Features** groups related work within versions. Opening this entry directly
does not choose your intended creation context; navigate through Products and
Versions when adding work to a specific version.

![Sprints menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-sprint-list.png)

**Sprints** lets you find the planning periods containing stories and tasks.
Enter a sprint to work with its planned work.

![Backlog menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-backlog.png)

**Backlog** shows work for planning and scheduling. Select the intended sprint
there before changing its work.

![Active Sprints menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-active-sprints.png)

**Active Sprints** brings ongoing sprint work together for day-to-day tracking.
Use its sprint selection and grouping controls to choose the work you see.

![Quality Board menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-tab-quality-board.png)

**Quality Board** provides a quality overview of planning data. Use it to
investigate issues rather than assuming that every warning is a missing feature.

![Planning breadcrumbs](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-breadcrumbs.png)

The **breadcrumb links below the main menu** take you back through the planning
hierarchy, for example from Features to its version or product. The final entry
identifies the current page and is not a link. The breadcrumbs describe your
navigation context, not every product/version currently selected in the table.

### Review history or change the theme

![Undo history toggle](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-action-history-button.png)

The **backward-clock icon**, with tooltip **Undo history**, opens or closes
recorded planning changes alongside the page. See **Recover changes** for the
scope used on this page and how to replay a change.

![Theme switcher](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-theme-toggle.png)

The **half-light, half-dark circle** at the upper right switches between
light and dark themes. Its tooltip describes the theme you can switch to.
The preference is remembered in this browser; help screenshots follow the theme.

### Manage your account and working time

![Account menu button](images/help/light/MainLayout/{{audience}}/main-layout-user-menu.png)

Open the **avatar and email menu at the upper right**. Its entries concern your
account or the server, not the feature row you last selected.
The screenshots use demonstration accounts; your name, email and avatar can differ.

![Expanded account menu](images/help/light/MainLayout/{{audience}}/main-layout-user-menu-popup.png)

#### Manage Availability

![Manage Availability menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-availability.png)

Record how much of your working time is available for planning and when that
availability changes. This contributes to capacity; it is not a list of tasks
assigned to you.

#### Manage Location

![Manage Location menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-location.png)

Maintain your country and region over time. Location determines which regional
public holidays apply to your working calendar.

#### Manage Off Days

![Manage Off Days menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-off-days.png)

Record absences such as vacation with their start/end dates and type.
Use this for personal time off rather than changing the reusable work-week definition.

#### Manage Work Week

![Manage Work Week menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-work-week.png)

Choose an existing work week and the date from which it applies to you.
Assignments let your schedule change over time; they do not edit the shared
definition used by other people.

#### View Profile

![View Profile menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-view-profile.png)

Review your user profile and its planning information. This is your own profile,
not the list of all server users.

#### About

![About menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-about.png)

View application and licence information, including the link to Kassandra's
source repository.

#### Logout

![Logout menu entry](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-user-menu-logout.png)

Sign out of Kassandra. Save unfinished forms before signing out. Your identity
provider can retain its own sign-in session; signing in again may reuse that account.
