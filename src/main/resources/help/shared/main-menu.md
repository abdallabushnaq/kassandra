### Navigate between planning pages

The **main menu across the top** switches between the available pages.
To work within a particular product and version, start from **Products** and
open that product's versions, then the intended version's features.
Opening **Features** directly from the main menu does not choose your intended
creation context for you.

The **breadcrumb links below the main menu** take you back through the planning
hierarchy. The final entry identifies the current page.

### Review history or change the theme

The **backward-clock icon**, with tooltip **Undo history**, opens or closes
recorded planning changes alongside the page. See **Recover changes** for the
scope used on this page and how to replay a change.

The **half-light, half-dark circle** at the upper right switches between
light and dark themes. Its tooltip describes the theme you can switch to.
The preference is remembered in this browser; help screenshots follow the theme.

### Manage your account and working time

Open the **avatar and email menu at the upper right**:

| Menu entry | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Manage Availability** | Manage your availability for planning. |
| **Manage Location** | Manage your location information. |
| **Manage Off Days** | Record time off that affects your working calendar. |
| **Manage Work Week** | Manage your work-week assignment. |
| **View Profile** | Review your user profile. |
| **About** | View application information. |
| **Logout** | Sign out of Kassandra. |

Administrators also see **Identity Providers**, **Manage User Groups**,
**Manage Users**, **Manage Work Weeks**, **Manage Settings** and **Audit**.
These lead to authentication, access and user management, work-week definitions,
server configuration and the audit log. They are administration pages, not
actions on the selected feature.
