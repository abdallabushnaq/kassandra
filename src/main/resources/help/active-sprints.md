**Active Sprints** brings sprint work together for day-to-day progress tracking.
Use Backlog for detailed planning and this board for finding and updating work.

Open a chapter for the task you need. **Expand all** opens every chapter;
**Collapse all** returns to the overview.

## Choose the work shown on the board

![Search board](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/search-field.png)

Search narrows board work as you type. This is plain-text board search,
not the AI-powered entity-list filter.

![User selector](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/user-selector.png)

Select users to focus on their assigned work.

![Sprint selector](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/sprint-selector.png)

Select the sprints you want to follow. Check the selected sprints rather than
assuming the board always shows just one feature.

![Grouping](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/grouping-mode-selector.png)

The grouping selector changes how cards are arranged, not their ownership or
assignments. The viewing selections are represented in the URL.

![Reset board filters](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/clear-filters-button.png)

Clear filters resets search, user and sprint selections and restores grouping
by Features. It does not remove tasks or clear their assignees.

## Work with cards

![Sprint board](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/active-sprints-board.png)

Inspect status columns and card information to find the next work to do.
Filtering and grouping may hide or rearrange cards without changing them.

![Task card title](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/task-card-title-.png)

Open a task through its card to inspect or edit the available task information.
Use the card's offered worklog controls to record time on the intended task,
not on a similarly named task in another sprint.

Changes made through task and worklog actions affect saved planning data.
Inspect error notifications before retrying. Use Backlog when you need to
restructure a story or change dependencies rather than just track progress.

## Compare progress with the schedule

![Gantt and burndown](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/active-sprints-gantt-burndown-container.png)

Use the combined chart to compare planned timing with recorded progress.
Missing assignments, estimates or worklogs can affect the displayed forecast.

![Open chart separately](images/help/light/ActiveSprints/open-active-sprints-gantt-burndown-chart-button.png)

The chart button opens a separate browser window. Allow popups if the window
does not appear. Opening or grouping a chart does not update tasks.

## Recover changes

![Planning history](images/help/light/MainLayout/main-layout-action-history-button.png)

{{include:undo-redo}}

History follows the products of the displayed sprints. When viewing several
products together, check the operation's product or return to Products to
locate the intended history.
